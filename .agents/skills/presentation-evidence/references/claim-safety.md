# Claim safety

| Safety | Meaning | Allowed use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Directly supportable with the cited source and framing. | Main talk. |
| B | Careful interpretation that stays qualified and caveated. | Main talk with cautious wording. |
| C | Context-dependent, contested, or definition-sensitive. Notes or backup only. | Notes or backup only. |
| D | Unsafe or unsupported. | Reject. |

In research-enhanced mode, review correlation versus causation, competing measures, stale values, and official or primary-source preference before assigning the level.
