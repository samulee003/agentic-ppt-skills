# STORY

- Memorable thesis: 被長者拒絕不是失敗，放棄才是。

## Audience change

- Starting state: new staff read a refusal at the door as personal failure and close the case
- Ending state: they expect refusals, and they have three opening lines and a follow-up plan

## Audience journey

- Why they should care: one in three new cases refused the first visit last year, and over half of those later accepted service
- What they should now believe, decide, or do: use acknowledge → small request → keep showing up
