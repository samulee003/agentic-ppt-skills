# PRESENTATION-BRIEF

## Playback

- 一個 45 分鐘、畀約 20 位新入職前線同工嘅工作坊，目標係佢哋下次被長者拒絕時唔即刻放棄，識得用「承認 → 細請求 → 陪伴」三步建立信任。

## Profile and deck type

- Profile: `training-zh-hant`
- Deck type: `training`
- Story structure: 4MAT (default for training)

## Audience

- Primary audience: about 20 frontline staff who joined this year, mostly social-work graduates
- Secondary audience: none
- What they already know: social-work theory; no home-visit experience

## Problem

- Decision or tension: new staff take a refusal at the door as failure and close the case
- Why it matters now: in the centre's 2025 review, 38 of 120 new cases refused the first home visit

## Outcome

- What should change after the presentation: when refused, they do not give up, and they can use the three steps on a first visit

## Promise

- What the presentation will help the audience do, decide, or understand: 45 分鐘後，你會有三句即刻用得嘅開場白，同一個被拒絕後唔放棄嘅計劃。

## Evidence threshold

- Required proof level: practice framework presented as the centre's own method; the only numbers are the centre's internal 2025 review, labelled as such
- Claims that need hard support: 38/120 and 21/38 (spreadsheet to follow); the "80%" claim is excluded unless a source is found

## Constraints

- Time (talk length, Q&A length): 45 min including a 15-minute role-play; no separate Q&A
- Venue and screen (room size, projector or screen share, aspect ratio): centre activity room, projector, 16:9
- Language on screen / language spoken: Traditional written Chinese on screen; Cantonese spoken
- Tone: warm, practical, never blaming staff
- Non-negotiables: refusal is normal; anonymise the case

## Deliverables

- Presenting deck / reading deck / both: presenting deck, plus a printable pocket card as the last slide
- Formats: `.pptx`, `.pdf`
- Must the `.pptx` be natively editable in PowerPoint? yes (reused next year)
- Where will the team edit it? PowerPoint
- Live file link (Google Slides or external tool), if any: none
- Speaker notes language: Cantonese
- Engine preference, if any: none

## Brand template

- Master or template file (`.potx`/`.pptx`) or brand guide: none; centre logo on the cover and the last slide
- What it fixes (palette, fonts, logo placement, title area): logo placement only

## Visual direction

- Theme: `sticker-pop`
- Overrides to the theme (palette, fonts, surfaces), if any: none
- Imagery: drawn shapes only; no photos of real elders
- Prohibited anti-patterns: text-only slides (the previous deck's problem)

## Success test

- Observable signal that the presentation worked: during the role-play every pair completes all three steps, observed by the supervisor

## Out of scope

- Topics to exclude: elder-abuse and safeguarding cases; mental-capacity assessment
- Questions to defer: case-management procedures after acceptance
